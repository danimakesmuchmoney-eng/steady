# Steady

A habit tracker with flexible weekly goals that adapt to your real life.

**Open the app:** https://danimakesmuchmoney-eng.github.io/steady/

Install it on your phone: open the link, then use "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone: Share button in Safari; Android: Install app).

Your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking. [Privacy policy](https://danimakesmuchmoney-eng.github.io/steady/privacy.html)
