Steady – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Steady is a habit tracker. We built it so your data stays with you.

What we collect: almost nothing. Steady has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Where your data lives: the habits and check-ins you enter are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases: if you subscribe to Steady Pro, Apple processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID and your purchase history, never your habits. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications: if you turn on daily reminders, Steady schedules them on your device. No server is involved.

Deleting your data: open Steady › Reminders › Delete all data, or delete the app.

Children: Steady does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes: if this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
